The Investment Thesis

We don't fund projects.
We finance infrastructure.

The global workforce crisis is not a talent problem. It is a systems coordination failure — training programs exist, employer demand exists, and worker motivation exists, but structural disconnects prevent people from accessing dignified, income-generating work at scale. This is true in Lagos. It is equally true in Louisville.

Arise Funds finances the infrastructure that resolves this failure: workforce development and technology companies with employer-integrated models, AI-enabled operations, and capital structures designed to generate returns as employer revenue scales. These are not training programs. They are human capital enterprises.

"We act as the initial derisker — providing the capital, operational infrastructure, and institutional systems that make early-stage workforce companies investable — before transitioning to long-term institutional investment partners."

The model we finance operates as a talent economy flywheel: a self-reinforcing system where every cohort builds capacity, generates revenue, and funds the next cycle. Training doesn't cost. It pays.

How We Work

An active investor model
built for global markets.

We don't write checks and wait. We deploy capital and operational infrastructure together — because in workforce markets anywhere in the world, one without the other rarely works.

01 — Capital

Structured entry into blended capital vehicles.

We structure and deploy blended capital — combining concessional funding, income-linked financing, and equity — calibrated to the institution's stage and risk profile. Capital is patient, structured around employer pipeline depth, not arbitrary timelines.

  • Human Capital Bonds — income-linked repayment tied to verified employment
  • First-loss concessional capital — absorbing early-stage risk before institutional investors enter
  • Equity structured around commercial revenue milestones, not training completions
  • 50% young women participation target — tracked and reported every cohort
02 — Infrastructure

Operational build-out that creates investable companies.

Alongside capital, we second talent directly into portfolio companies to build the institutional infrastructure that larger investors require. HR, governance, compliance, financial controls, and M&E systems — built from the ground up, to institutional standard.

  • Full HR, payroll, and statutory compliance infrastructure — Nigeria, Sierra Leone, and beyond
  • Governance and organizational design built to DFI audit standards from day one
  • LMS deployment, curriculum standardization, and employer integration systems
  • AI readiness assessment and deployment — ensuring portfolio companies are future-ready
03 — Scale

Transition to institutional capital at scale.

We position portfolio companies for long-term institutional capital — DFIs, development banks, sovereign wealth funds — by building the data, governance, and verified outcome infrastructure they require. We de-risk the entry. They provide the scale.

  • M&E frameworks aligned to World Bank, MCC, and DFI reporting standards
  • Employment verification + 12-month retention tracking — not completion rates
  • Government and multilateral partnership structuring
  • Investor-ready documentation: audit-ready financials, compliance records, outcome data

The ICBM Model

Integrated Competency-Based
Methodology

ICBM is a 7-stage workforce development flywheel — not a training program. It is economic infrastructure that, once deployed at scale, becomes self-sustaining. The framework has been deployed across Nigeria and Sierra Leone and is designed for rapid adaptation to new markets with existing employer networks — across industries, geographies, and stages of economic development.

01
Entry Point
Discover

AI-driven aptitude assessment and pathway matching — directing talent correctly before training begins.

02
Barrier Removal
Prepare

Removing structural barriers to participation — devices, connectivity, financing — before training begins.

03
Skills Development
Train

Competency-based training across five digital economy pathways — building job-ready skills, not theoretical credentials.

04
Credentialing
Certify

Globally recognized certifications that employers trust and that hold their value across borders.

05
Employment Creation
Employ

Verified employment placement — into BPO operations, government contracts, and private sector partnerships. Training leads directly to income-generating work.

06
Career Progression
Advance

Graduates advance into higher-value roles — analysts, engineers, managers, and entrepreneurs — building long-term career trajectories.

07
The Flywheel Effect
Reinvest

Graduate repayments and employer revenue fund the next cohort. The system becomes self-sustaining at scale.

First Portfolio Investment

The model,
proven.

SBTS Group LLC is the first company financed and operationally supported by Arise Funds — and the live demonstration that Workforce Infrastructure Investing works. In January 2026, the first cohort of ICBM graduates began earning income in verified digital economy roles.

With active campuses in Abuja, Enugu, and Freetown and a current footprint spanning eight countries, SBTS Group delivers the Intelligent Capacity Building Model: a seven-stage talent economy flywheel with employer commitments secured before training begins — with international expansion actively in development.

Alongside capital, Arise Funds has built SBTS Group's complete HR infrastructure, governance frameworks, payroll systems, and financial controls — from the ground up.

SBTS Group is not just being financed. It is being built.

Arise Funds targets 50% young women participation across every cohort — tracked from intake through employment and 12-month retention. Income progression and financial inclusion outcomes are reported against ILO workforce development standards and form part of every institutional partner reporting package.

Arise Funds actively builds and expands the employer network that absorbs ICBM graduates — including BPO operators, government outsourcing contracts, technology companies, and multinational partners seeking verified, certified digital talent in high-growth markets. Employer partnerships are a core part of how we structure every deployment.
